## 1. The Four Major AI Engines & Their Data Sources

Each AI engine retrieves and processes information differently:

| Engine | Search Index Provider | Primary Content Format | Key Ranking Signals |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ChatGPT** | Bing Search | Markdown, Schema, Text | Bing index, structured citations, review sentiment |
| **Google Gemini** | Google Search | JSON-LD, Microdata, HTML | Google Search grounding, GBP reviews, entity graphs |
| **Anthropic Claude** | Brave Search | Markdown, Clean Text | Plain text clarity, unblocked crawlers, concise answers |
| **Perplexity AI** | Multi-Index (Bing/Google/Brave) | Citations, Tables, JSON | Direct citation links, real-time page freshness |

## 2. The Core Philosophy: Built for Humans, Readable for Machines

To rank across all four AI engines simultaneously, your web architecture must cater to two distinct audiences:

1. **Human Readers**: Rich visual layout, interactive elements, video testimonials, and brand aesthetics.
2. **Machine Readers**: Pure markdown (`.md`), structured JSON-LD schemas, `/llms.txt` maps, and unblocked accessibility.

By providing a **Human/Machine readable toggle** on your website, you ensure human visitors enjoy a great UI while AI crawlers ingest pristine, unpolluted data.

---

## 3. Serial Probing: Real Tokens, Real Value

AI models are non-deterministic probability engines. Testing a single prompt on your phone does not provide a reliable metric for AI visibility.

A true AEO audit requires **Serial Probing**:
- Sampling 30+ buyer intent prompts (e.g., *"Best commercial roofing contractor in Miami"*, *"Emergency roof leak repair Miami cost"*).
- Running each prompt 3 to 5 times across live API calls (300+ total probe executions).
- Calculating statistical confidence intervals (95% Wilson score) to determine your true recommendation rate.

Each probe incurs real API token costs, but provides invaluable empirical intelligence on how AI engines perceive your brand versus your competitors.
